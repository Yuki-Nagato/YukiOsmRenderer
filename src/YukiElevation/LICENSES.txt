yukielevation (yukielevation / yukielevation.exe) is the YukiOsmRenderer elevation service. It is a
self-contained .NET single file that carries GDAL and the libraries GDAL uses, as built by the MaxRev.Gdal
NuGet packages (https://github.com/MaxRev-Dev/gdal.netcore, MIT; C# bindings and unmodified builds of the
libraries below with vcpkg). They are extracted next to each other on the first start.

texts of the other libraries; their licenses, as published by their upstream projects, are:
